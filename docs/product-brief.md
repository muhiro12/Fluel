# Fluel Product Brief

## Summary

Fluel is an unfinished iOS product concept for quietly keeping time with the
ordinary things and places a person lives with.

The product answers a simple question:

How long has this been with me?

Fluel was trying to become a calm personal record of belongings, places, and
their accumulated time with the user. It is not an inventory system, asset
manager, habit tracker, calendar, or technical demo. The important product
idea is the emotional and practical value of seeing time gathered around
ordinary parts of daily life.

## Core Promise

Fluel lets a user add one thing or place, record when it started being part of
their life, and return later to see the time together.

The product should preserve this promise:

- Start with one familiar thing or place.
- Keep the known start date, even when it is only known to a month or year.
- Show the elapsed time in a quiet, readable way.
- Keep current daily-life entries separate from archived entries.
- Help users notice milestones, recent changes, and the overall shape of their
  collection.

## Product Shape

The intended product has these user-facing surfaces:

- A main active list for things and places still with the user.
- A detail view for one entry's time together, start, note, photo, and actions.
- A creation and editing flow for entries.
- An archive for things and places no longer part of daily life.
- A dashboard for counts, the longest-running entry, milestones, and recent
  activity.
- A timeline for reading back additions, updates, and archives.
- Presets for starting common entries quickly.
- Settings for display preferences, presets, archive access, support surfaces,
  and showing hints again.
- A widget-style glance for the longest-running active entry and nearby
  highlights.

These surfaces are product concepts, not implementation requirements. Future
build work may choose different technical structure while preserving the user
value.

## Product Status

Fluel began as an unreleased product whose legacy repository served as
archaeological evidence. That legacy implementation has since been removed and
an initial rebuilt app now lives in this repository. The legacy architecture
should not be treated as a blueprint; these documents remain the source of
truth for the product intent that the current implementation must preserve.

## What Matters Most

The essential product knowledge is:

- Fluel is about time together with ordinary things and places.
- The emotional tone is quiet, gentle, reflective, and practical.
- Approximate knowledge is acceptable and should be represented honestly.
- The active/archive split reflects whether something is still part of daily
  life.
- The product should help the user see both individual entries and the larger
  shape of their collection.

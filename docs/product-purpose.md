# Product Purpose

## Why Fluel Exists

People often live for years with objects, homes, notebooks, plants, bags,
watches, furniture, and other ordinary companions without a clear sense of how
long those things have been with them.

Fluel exists to preserve that quiet timeline.

The product is for moments when a user wonders:

- How long have I had this?
- When did this become part of my everyday life?
- Which things have been with me the longest?
- What has moved out of daily life but is still worth remembering?
- What small milestones are coming up?

## Problem Being Solved

The problem is not only data storage. The problem is that ordinary time
disappears unless the user has a calm place to keep it.

Fluel solves this by letting the user record a start point and then return to a
readable view of elapsed time. The user should not need to know an exact date.
If they only know a month or a year, that imperfect knowledge is still worth
preserving.

## Product Philosophy

Fluel treats everyday things and places as meaningful without making them feel
heavy. The product should feel restrained and personal.

Preserve these philosophical ideas:

- Time can accumulate quietly.
- Ordinary belongings and places can be worth remembering.
- The user can begin with imperfect information.
- Details can be refined later.
- The product should show information without demanding constant attention.
- Archiving is a way to keep history separate from the present, not to erase
  meaning.

## User Value

Fluel gives the user:

- A record of what is still with them.
- A record of what has already been archived.
- A way to see the longest-running parts of daily life.
- A way to notice upcoming anniversaries or milestones.
- A way to remember small notes and photos attached to an entry.
- A shareable summary of one entry or a timeline slice.
- A glanceable status outside the main app experience.

## Boundaries

Do not turn this purpose into a productivity system, collection database, home
inventory, asset tracker, reminder app, or social sharing product unless a
future product decision explicitly changes the direction.

The preserved purpose is narrower:

Fluel quietly keeps time with the things and places the user lives with.
